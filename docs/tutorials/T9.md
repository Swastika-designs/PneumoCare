# T9 — Healthcare Strategic Planning Part 2

**Where in the app:** Operations Analytics → **Utilisation & scenarios** tab (`/operations?tab=planning`)  
**Status:** verified - 3/3 workflow checks passed against the live API (2026-10-28, `scripts/verify_tutorials.py`)

## Objective

Analyse demand patterns and resource utilisation, and compare what-if scenarios.

## Key concepts

- **Demand patterns.** Day × hour heat-map; demand by year and encounter class; year-over-year growth for the two most recent complete years.
- **Resource utilisation.** Provider workload (encounters per provider per active year, share handled by the busiest 10). Bed utilisation = occupied bed-days / (beds × days in month), with an 85% target line.
- **Scenario analysis.** Baseline = clinic demand over the last 12 months. Each scenario sets a demand change %, staff FTE and optionally minutes per encounter. Outputs: workload, capacity, utilisation, unmet hours, FTE needed at 85% utilisation, and projected cost.

## Dataset

Synthea encounters (2016+). The bed capacity is a user-editable assumption.

## Methodology

Crosstab of start times → yearly pivot by class → provider-year averages → daily bed-occupancy series from long-stay intervals → deterministic scenario formulas.

## Implementation

- Code: `backend/app/services/operations.py` (`operations`, `scenarios`), `frontend/src/pages/Operations.tsx` (`PlanningTab`)
- API: `GET /api/operations/utilization?beds=`, `POST /api/operations/scenarios`

## Outputs

Heat-map, stacked demand bars, growth table, workload distribution, bed-utilisation chart, scenario chart and table with status badges.

## Results (actual run, 26 Oct 2026)

215 providers average 2.4 encounters per provider-year, and the top 10 handle 25% of the workload. Ambulatory visits fell 23% between the last two complete years. Doubling demand exactly doubles workload and utilisation (checked in the tests).

## How to run it

Operations Analytics → **Utilisation & scenarios**. Move the beds slider, edit or add scenarios, and press **Compare scenarios**.

Prerequisites: the backend and frontend are running (see README) and the dataset has been downloaded.

## Limitations

Synthea timestamps are simulated and stored in UTC, so the time-of-day pattern does not reflect real opening hours. The scenarios are linear and deterministic (no queueing or variability).
