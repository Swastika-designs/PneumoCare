# T7 — Patient Prediction for Improved Healthcare Staffing

**Where in the app:** Operations Analytics → **Volume & staffing** tab (`/operations?tab=forecast`)  
**Status:** verified - 5/5 workflow checks passed against the live API (2026-10-28, `scripts/verify_tutorials.py`)

## Objective

Forecast patient volume and translate it into a staffing estimate.

## Key concepts

- **Why a workload-based alternative.** Synthea has no staff rosters or staffing labels, so staff numbers cannot be predicted directly. Instead we predict **patient volume** (which has real labels: the observed encounter counts) and convert it to **workload** (encounters × median observed encounter duration) and full-time equivalents (FTE).
- **Time-series features.** Lags 1–3 (monthly) or 1–4 (weekly), a rolling mean, seasonal sine/cosine of the month or week, and a trend index.
- **Time-based split.** The most recent 20% of periods are the test set. Random splits would leak the future into training.
- **Baselines.** Naive (next = last) and moving average. A model is useful only if it beats them.
- **Metrics.** MAE, RMSE, MAPE (with denominator max(y, 1)).
- **FTE.** Required FTE = workload hours / (hours per FTE per period × target utilisation, 0.85).

## Dataset

Synthea encounters from 2016 onwards (3,935 encounters, 101 patients). Inpatient, SNF and hospice stays are excluded from clinic workload, because they consume beds rather than clinician hours.

## Methodology

1. Aggregate encounters into weekly or monthly counts (optionally for one encounter class); drop the incomplete last period.
2. Build lag and season features; split by time.
3. Fit Ridge regression and a random forest (300 trees); compare with the baselines.
4. Re-fit the best model on all data; forecast recursively for the chosen horizon.
5. Convert the forecast to workload hours and required FTE.

## Results (actual run, 26 Oct 2026)

Monthly, all classes (129 periods; 100 train / 26 test): MAE naive 6.62, moving average 6.46, Ridge 5.74, **random forest 5.71** (MAPE 19%). Average monthly volume is about 36 clinic encounters, with a median duration of 31 minutes, so only about 0.15 FTE is needed. A real clinic would have far higher volumes.

## How to run it

Operations Analytics → **Volume & staffing**. Switch between monthly and weekly, pick an encounter class, and move the horizon and FTE-hours sliders.

Prerequisites: the backend and frontend are running (see README) and the dataset has been downloaded.

## Limitations

Only 111 synthetic patients, so the error estimates are noisy. Recursive forecasts accumulate error. There are no holiday or epidemic features. Durations are simulated. FTE is a planning estimate, not a roster.
